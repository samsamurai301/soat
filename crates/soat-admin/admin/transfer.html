Import and export

Moving from another shop? Upload its exports as they are: Shopify’s product export (Products › Export, “Plain CSV file”) and customer export, WooCommerce’s product export (Products › Export), or Shopware’s product and customer exports (Settings › Import/Export, default profiles). Product images come along: SOAT fetches the links in the export and keeps the images itself.

Products

Export products

A row per variant; rows with the same handle are one product. A handle the shop has updates that product, and its variants are matched by SKU. Empty cells change nothing; stock sets the units on hand; images takes links to the product's images, separated by spaces, fetched once each. Columns: {{ product_columns }}.

Export customers

An email the shop has updates that customer. Columns: {{ customer_columns }}.
