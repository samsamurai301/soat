# E-invoices

Every invoice and credit note SOAT issues can be downloaded as an
electronic invoice after EN 16931, the European standard. Public buyers in
the EU must accept it, Germany requires it between businesses from 2025
(receiving) and 2027/2028 (sending), and Belgium sends B2B invoices over
Peppol from 2026.

| Format | `?format=` | Syntax | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| XRechnung 3.0 | `xrechnung` (default) | UN/CEFACT CII | German public buyers, German B2B |
| ZUGFeRD / Factur-X, profile EN 16931 | `cii` (also `zugferd`, `facturx`) | CII | German and French B2B; the XML that goes into the PDF |
| Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 | `peppol` (also `ubl`) | UBL 2.1 | the Peppol network (Belgium, the Nordics, ...) |
| ZUGFeRD / Factur-X PDF | `pdf` (also `zugferd-pdf`, `facturx-pdf`) | PDF/A-3b with the CII inside | buyers who read the PDF and book the XML |

## Downloading

```http
GET /api/invoices/{id}/e-invoice?format=xrechnung&buyer_reference=04011000-1234512345-06
```

answers the XML as a download (`invoices:read`). `buyer_reference` is BT-10:
public buyers in Germany give a Leitweg-ID, which must be on the invoice;
without it, the order number is used. The buyer downloads their own with
the order's token:

```http
GET /api/storefront/orders/{order_id}/invoices/{id}/e-invoice?format=cii
x-soat-order-token: soat_order_...
```

In the admin, an order's page lists its invoices and credit notes with a
printable page and the downloads.

## The hybrid PDF

`format=pdf` answers `{number}.pdf`: the invoice laid out for people, with
the EN 16931 CII attached as `factur-x.xml` (relationship `Alternative`), so
a person reads it and the buyer's bookkeeping imports the XML. It is
PDF/A-3b: fonts embedded and subset (Liberation Sans, SIL Open Font
License, in `crates/soat-core/assets/fonts`), an sRGB output intent, and XMP
metadata naming the Factur-X profile. `soat_core::pdf` is the writer (text,
lines, attachments; no dependencies beyond zlib), `soat_core::facturx` the
layout. Long invoices break across pages, each with a footer.

## Checking before sending

```http
GET /api/invoices/{id}/e-invoice/check?format=xrechnung
```

```json
{ "format": "xrechnung", "ready": false, "problems": [
  { "rule": "BR-DE-6", "message": "XRechnung needs the seller's phone number: set it in the invoice address." }
] }
```

names, by the rule's own name, what a receiver's validator would reject:
the seller's phone and email and the buyer's reference for XRechnung,
electronic addresses for Peppol, VAT IDs for reverse charge and VAT, an
address for the buyer, totals that do not add up. It checks what a shop's
details can break, not the whole schematron, so a receiver may still find
more. The admin order page shows the XRechnung problems under each invoice.

Issued invoices never change: details added to the invoice settings later
(a phone, an email) show on invoices issued after.

## What goes in

- **Seller** (from the invoice settings when the invoice was issued): the
  legal name, address, phone (in the address), email (also the electronic
  address, scheme `EM`), VAT ID and tax number.
- **Buyer**: the billing (else shipping) address, their email, their VAT ID.
- **Lines**: each with its net amount after discounts, quantity (unit `C62`)
  and VAT category and rate; shipping is a line of its own.
- **VAT** per category and rate: `S` above 0%, `AE` for reverse charge
  (`VATEX-EU-AE`), `G` for exports outside the EU (`VATEX-EU-G`), `Z` for
  other 0% amounts.
- **Payment**: the first valid IBAN in the bank details (credit transfer,
  code 58, the invoice number as the reference), what was paid (an invoice
  of a paid order, or a credit note of a refund, is due 0.00).
- **Credit notes** are type 381 with positive amounts and name the invoice
  they credit.

The XML is generated by `soat_core::einvoice` (`xml`, `check`), which knows
nothing of the database, so apps and tools can build documents of their own.
